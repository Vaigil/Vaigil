# The Aertun mark

A capital A whose crossbar is a streamline: it comes out from behind the
left leg, passes over the right one, and leaves rising, cut at an angle like
the trailing edge of a wing. Air through the tunnel, and lift out of it.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `aertun-wordmark.svg` | the name, with the mark as its A — the primary logo |
| `aertun-lockup.svg` | the mark beside the full name, where the A must read as a letter on its own |
| `aertun-mark.svg` | the mark alone: avatars, small spaces |
| `aertun-icon.svg`, `aertun-icon-*.png` | the mark on its tile: favicon, app icon |
| `aertun-social.png` | 1280×640 card for link previews |

Files without a suffix follow the viewer's light or dark preference; `-light`
and `-dark` are fixed for a known background.

Colours: ink `#0A1F44` (light backgrounds) and `#E8EEF8` (dark), the
streamline a gradient from `#00B4D8` to `#1DE9B6`, the tile `#0E2A5A` to
`#071634`.

Keep the streamline's left end hidden behind the left leg and its right end
cut, never rounded or turned into an arrowhead; keep the gap where it crosses
the right leg. Leave clear space of at least the width of one leg on every
side.

The letters are outlines of the interface's own typeface (see NOTICE). The
name and the mark have had a preliminary search only; a formal trademark
search comes before any filing.
